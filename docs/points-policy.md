# Community Points Policy

Status: Draft — beta only
Last updated: 2026-11-03

## What points are

Community Points are a non-monetary recognition and access system for the beta.

They are:

- non-contractual;
- non-transferable;
- not redeemable for cash;
- not equity, wages, securities, or investment;
- best effort;
- subject to change;
- beta-only.

## What points are not

- Not a cryptocurrency or token.
- Not a claim on revenue or profits.
- Not a guaranteed claim on future compute.
- Not a store of value.
- Not a promise of unlimited inference.

## Earning points

Points may be granted for verified work:

- hardware benchmark;
- accepted evaluation jobs;
- verified LoRA fine-tune jobs;
- accepted data contributions;
- documentation, moderation, or community work, at admin discretion.

Points are issued after verification, not on job start.

## Spending points

Points may be spent on:

- inference through the beta API;
- priority access, at admin discretion;
- early access to models, at admin discretion.

## Rates

Initial rates are placeholders and will be calibrated after the first beta:

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Benchmark | One-time grant |
| Evaluation job | Per accepted task |
| LoRA fine-tune | Per verified job |
| Inference | Per 1K tokens, model-specific |

Rates may change during the beta.

## Expiry and administration

- Points may expire at the end of the beta or after a published period.
- Admin may adjust points for fraud, abuse, or errors.
- Admin adjustments are logged.
- Disputes are handled by the project team during the beta.

## No transfers

- Points cannot be transferred between users.
- Points cannot be sold.
- There is no exchange.
- There is no cash-out.

## Future changes

If the project later introduces paid credits, they will be separate from Community Points. Points will not be converted to cash or paid credits without a new policy and legal review.
