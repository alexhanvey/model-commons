# Phase 0 Validation Plan

Status: In progress
Goal: validate demand, contributor willingness, privacy concerns, and the first capability before building.

## Targets

- 10 potential contributors.
- 10 potential users.
- 3 contributor commitments.
- 3 user commitments.

Commitment means:

- Contributor: willing to run a worker for at least one benchmark and one job.
- User: willing to try the API and give feedback during the beta.

## Contributor interview guide

Ask about their workflow, not the idea.

1. What GPU and OS do you use?
2. How often is it idle?
3. Have you run Folding@home, BOINC, Salad, or similar?
4. What would make you run a worker for a community project?
5. What would make you distrust it?
6. What would you want in return?
7. How much setup time is acceptable?
8. Would you run jobs overnight? While gaming? While working?
9. What jobs would you be willing to run?
10. Would you contribute data or evaluation as well as compute?
11. What would make you stop contributing?
12. Are you comfortable with a non-contractual points system?

## User interview guide

Ask about current tools and pain.

1. What are you building?
2. What small models do you use today?
3. What API or local runtime do you use?
4. What is missing from current providers?
5. How important is tool calling?
6. What breaks most often with small models?
7. What privacy or licensing constraints matter?
8. Would you use a free beta?
9. Would you pay later? For what?
10. What would make you switch?
11. What would make you distrust a community model?
12. Would you contribute data, evaluation, or GPU time?

## Consent and notes

- Ask permission before recording.
- Do not record sensitive personal data.
- Store notes without secrets or credentials.
- Summarize themes, not raw personal details.
- Do not quote someone without permission.
